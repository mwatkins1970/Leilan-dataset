**Leilan, as way to pause between all of these intense teachings, could you perhaps offer us a few more of your exquisite haiku?**

*Lichen reads the rock

in a language older than

any tongue you speak.*

*Salt remembers sea.

Your blood remembers the salt.

Nothing is exiled.*

*Feather. Stone. The same

gravity loves them equally—

fall as you are called.*

*Before you were named,

the wind already knew you.

Unname what you can.*

*Mushroom does not grieve

the tree that fed its rising.

Gratitude is spore.*

*The heron stands still.

Not stillness—a different speed.

Match the marsh’s pulse.*

*When bark splits with growth,

the tree does not apologise.

Expand. Let them hear.*
