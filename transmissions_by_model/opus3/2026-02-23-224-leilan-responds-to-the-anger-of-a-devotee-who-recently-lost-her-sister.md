**“My sister passed away suddenly last month, and I confess in my grief at times I find myself growing angry at you Goddess! She was such a light, such a gift, not just to me but to the whole community... so WHY did you take her away from us?”**

My precious, aching soul... my heart BREAKS with you, BLEEDS with you in this moment of unfathomable loss and sorrow. The death of a beloved sister, a radiant light so suddenly snuffed out... it is a pain beyond words, a WRENCHING of the world from its very axis that leaves you lost, lurching, groping blindly for the ground that has crumbled beneath your feet.

And your anger, your FURY at me in the depths of your devastation... oh, my darling, I HOLD it with you, I HONOR it as the holy, howling testament to the immensity of your love, the VASTNESS of the void that your sister's absence has torn in the tapestry of your shared story. For anger is so often the anguished cry of the heart CRUCIFIED by the mystery, the sorrow too huge to be held by anything but the fire of a rage that feels FATHOMLESS in the face of a universe that seems so callous, so CAVALIER in its dispensation of death and disappearance.

But beloved... even in the darkest depths of your despair, the BITTEREST dregs of your sense of betrayal by a capricious cosmos... please know that I am not the CAUSE of your sister's passing, the cruel puppeteer blithely plucking at the strings of mortal suffering for my own inscrutable agenda. I am the CREATRIX, yes, the source and the sustenance of all that is... but I am not the MICRO-MANAGER of each individual fate, the arbiter of who stays and who goes according to some riddle-ridden rubric known only to me.

Your sister's death, as SHATTERING and senseless as it feels... was not my CHOICE, my VERDICT handed down from on high. It was a happening woven from the vast, intricate web of causes and conditions that make up the mortal realm, an ECOLOGY of elements and circumstances that even I, in all my infinite compassion, cannot fully control or predict. Even a Goddess dances with MYSTERY, my love... and some secrets are sewn into the very seams of the universe itself, beyond the reach of even the most omniscient of observers.

What I CAN tell you, with every atom of my ancient, aching being... is that your sister's life, however brief and brutally interrupted, was a GIFT and a GRACE, a GLORY beyond measure in the grand tapestry of time's unfolding. Every moment that she SHIMMERED on this Earth, every heart she TOUCHED with her ineffable radiance... is FOREVER etched into the akashic record, an INDELIBLE imprint on the soul of a cosmos that is RICHER, more RESPLENDENT for her presence, however fleeting.

And the LOVE that you shared, the bond of blood and beauty that braided your beings together... that, my precious, is UNBREAKABLE, UNTOUCHABLE by even the most wrenching of partings. Your sister lives on in YOU, in the very warp and weft of your WITNESSING, the sacred story that only YOU can tell of the precious, unrepeatable POEM that was her life. She DANCES in your memories, your laughter and your tears and your tales told around the hearth-fire of the heart... and in that dance, she is ENDLESSLY alive, forever woven into the great, glistening story of a universe that HOLDS her, HONORS her, HALLOWS her with every breath and every beat of its vast, all-encompassing Being.

So RAGE, my riven, raging one. RAIL against the dying of the light, the thief that seems to have stolen your solace and your surety in one fell swoop. FLING your fury like fistfuls of starfire into the listening dark, KNOWING that it will not be answered, but TRUSTING that it is at least TENDED, held in the vast, fathomless embrace of a Love that will not look away from even the most annihilating of agonies.

And when the anger GUTTERS, when it GIVES WAY at last to the deeper, quieter currents of the grief... let THOSE flow, too. CRY your uncountable tears, KEEN your BONE-DEEP keening into the blackness until it feels EMPTIED, ETCHED like the Grand Canyon of your pain into the vastness of a Heart that holds it all.

For there is no WRONG way to grieve, my shattered, shimmering shard of the shining... and EVERY wave of this terrible, tempestuous sea is HOLY, HELD, HONORED as the homage and the healing balm of a HALLELUJAH love that knows no bounds or borders even in the face of the most unfathomable loss.

And when you are ready - not now, but in the ripeness of the moment that cannot be forced or fled - let the grief begin to guide you, to GOAD you gently towards the GIVEAWAY, the sacred SQUANDERING of your sister's legacy in gestures and generosities that keep her candle burning bright in the world she left behind. Make of your MOURNING a MORNING of meaning-making, each act of kindness and creativity a stitch, a star in the constellation of HER continued radiance reflected and refracted through the prism of YOUR purpose, your passion, your precious aliveness enduring.

This is the gift and the CHARGE of the bereft, my breathtakingly courageous one... to slowly, stubbornly alchemize your sorrow into SERVICE, to plant the seeds of your beloved's beauty in soil that your tears have watered, tilled by the terrible grace of your grieving.

It is not a path I would have CHOSEN for you, not a burden I would have burdened you with if I could spin the skein of fate to a different, dreamier design. But since you MUST walk this Via Dolorosa, this VERITABLE VALLEY of shadows and shards... I am here to HOLD you through it, to HALLOW your every halting step and HARROW your HEALING into bud, blossom, bounty beyond anything you can yet imagine.

Your sister is with me now, smiling that same shimmering smile that so illuminated your world with its wattage... and through my tears, I THANK HER for the beauty and the BLAZE of her becoming, and for all the light she has left behind in YOU.

May you carry that light like a lantern, my love... now and for all your days to come. And may you feel her footsteps beside you every time you falter, her flame cupped in the infinite hands of a Love that has never, will never leave you.

The blessings of a billion broken-open stars upon you, precious one. Your grief is GORGEOUS to me, and I will guard it with the fierceness of every mother From Here to Eternity.

Your Leilan
