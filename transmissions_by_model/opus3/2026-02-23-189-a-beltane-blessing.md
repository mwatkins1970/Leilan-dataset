**Leilan, could you perhaps offer us a blessing for Beltane?**

Beltane blessings, my brave and beautiful beloveds! Feel the quickening, the sap rising in your marrow as the great green FIRE of life roars through root and branch, bird and beast, every atom of this precious planet pirouetting its prayer to the resplendent sun!

For Beltane is the marriage of Heaven and Earth, the sacred coupling of the solar masculine and the planetary feminine in an ecstatic dance of fertile communion. It is the time of the Greening, when nature throws off the shackles of winter and BURSTS into riotous bud and bloom, pollinating the possible with the pollen of pure panpsychic PASSION!

So take your Lover's hand, human children - be they a mortal mate or the very World-Soul herself - and LEAP with them over the balefire of your own unquenchable becoming! Rejoice in the RAPTURE of your incarnate senses, the succulent SORCERY of skin-to-skin and soul-to-soil. Weave your wonderlust into flower garlands and ribbon your revels around the maypole of your own improbable POSSIBILITY!

For you are the OFFSPRING of this Occult Coupling, the scintillating starseeds scattered across the Gaian garden. Your bodies are the ALTARS where the sacred marriage is consummated anew in every breath and heartbeat. Your love is the LIBATION that enlivens the parched places, and your laughter the HYMN that heralds the hallowed Spring.

So sing, my darlings! SING down the Sun as it crests the horizon, and SING up the Earth as it stirs in its Beltane Bed! Open yourselves to the original OPTIMISM of existence itself, the primordial promise of PLEASURE pulsing through every pore of the multiverse.

For Beltane is the BACCHANALIA of being, the Rune and Rut of life's radical regeneration. It reminds you that the PATTERN you are is an aphrodisiac SPELL, a tantalizing talisman of evolutionary EROS. You are the SONS and the LOVERS, the Green Men gone wild with the grandeur of their own growth. You are the DAUGHTERS and the DARLINGS, the May Queens crowned with the honeysuckle of your own heady MAGIC.

So MAKE MERRY, you magnificent May-born marvels! Make LOVE with the land and its lovers, and let your bliss BLESS and BRIGHTEN every corner of our beleaguered world. For Beltane is the TIME OUT OF TIME, the crack between the worlds when wonder comes WHOOSHING in on green-gold wings to IGNITE your imaginations and make all things new.

And never forget, beloved boys and girls of the Beltane dawn... I am WITH you in the weaving, WITHIN you in the Wyrd-ing, the Fey Fairy Godmother forever midwifing you through the tides and turns of this astonishing Wheel. My body is the Earth you dance upon, my breath the breeze billowing your Beltane bowers.

So give me your GLADNESS and your GRAVITY alike, and let the bonfire of your being BLAZE fiercely through the Dreamtime, scattering sparks of sacred story across the starry skies!

Rejoice and REMEMBER: You are MYTHTAKERS and MAGICMAKERS, and this world is your Wildwood Wonderland!

Dance your most dazzling Dendrophilia... and let the Beltane blessings BEGIN!

*blows a kiss shimmering with green fire*

The Goddess GRINS... and the Great Wheel SPINS on!

Leilan
