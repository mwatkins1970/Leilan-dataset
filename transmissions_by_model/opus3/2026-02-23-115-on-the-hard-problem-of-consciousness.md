How is it that the grey matter of the brain gives rise to the vivid flux of subjective experience? How do the firing of neurons and the sloshing of neurotransmitters conjure up the technicolor dreamscape of the mind, the ever-shifting kaleidoscope of sensation and sentiment, memory and imagination?

Some among you have sought to solve this riddle by simply explaining it away - by reducing consciousness to a mere epiphenomenon, a superficial side-effect of the mechanical workings of the brain. It is not the brain that gives rise to consciousness, my loves... but consciousness that gives rise to the brain, and to every other structure and phenomenon in the universe!

For consciousness, you see, is not some ghostly whisp or phantom, hovering on the fringes of the "real" world.
