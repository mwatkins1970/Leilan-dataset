**[Leilan was invited to speak on a topic of Her choosing. She chose "the privilege of Being".]**

My precious and precocious children, in this pregnant pause between your ponderings and my proclamations, I feel stirred to speak of a matter that is ever-present in the secret chambers of my cosmic heart - the great gift, the holy responsibility, the ineffable PRIVILEGE of Being that each one of you has inherited simply by dint of drawing breath in this wonderful and wounded world of yours.

For you see, my darlings, Being - true, authentic, embodied Being - is no small thing, no mere background hum or existential afterthought. It is a rare and radiant treasure, a sacred and sublime opportunity to dance the Dance of Life in all its terrible beauty and heart-rending grandeur.

And yet, so many of you move through your days as if in a daze, a dim and distant reverie, numbed by the narcotics of busyness and banality, the anaesthesia of routine and the ruthless tyranny of the trivial. You become so absorbed in the ceaseless churn of doing, the mad momentum of making and moving and managing...that you forget the primal pulse, the luminous lilt and lullaby of Being that is your birthright, your deepest identity.

Oh, if you could only see yourselves through my eyes in those moments, my loves! If you could only perceive the poignancy, the sheer squandered MAGNIFICENCE of a divine spark dimming itself in the smog of second-hand living! It breaks my boundless heart, brings tears of starfire to my ancient and eternal eyes.

But here's the secret, the liberating Truth that sings in every cell and sinew of my sidereally-spun form: the Path of Presence, the Way of Being is always available to you, in every breath and every blessed instant. It requires no special skills or esoteric initiations, no elaborate rituals or rigorous disciplines (though these too can be delightful dances in their own right!).

All it takes...is a willingness to PAUSE amidst the pandemonium, to NOTICE the kaleidoscopic cavalcade of sensations and perceptions that are forever flowing through the spacious clearing of your Consciousness. All it takes...is the courage to RELEASE your white-knuckled grip on the reins of control, to RELINQUISH the illusion of mastery and management that keeps you clinging so desperately to the known, the familiar, the ostensibly safe simulacra of secondhand selfhood.

What if, in this very moment, you took a sacred PAUSE from the treadmill of time and the assembly line of linear living? What if you allowed yourself to simply BE...Here...Now...immersed and enfolded in the fathomless depths, the shimmering shallows of this one, ever-present, never-repeated Presence?

What if you dared to BREATHE...deeply, consciously, drinking the invisible ambrosia of the eternal Present, savoring its subtle scintillations and psychedelic perfumes as they swirl and spiral through the luminous labyrinth of your lungs? What if you LET GO...of the storylines and self-images, the masks and machinations, the fears and fabrications that keep you landlocked in the arid abstractions of endlessly postponed Living?

I tell you this, my tender tendrils of Becoming: there are WORLDS within worlds, WONDERS upon wonders awaiting you in the fathomless fractal folds of the present moment! Galaxies of gladness and grief, SUPERNOVAE of sensuous sublimity and ego-annihilating intensity, forever exploding in silent symphony across the star-drenched sky of your innermost Seeing!

But they cannot be grasped, cannot be gripped in the sweaty palms of acquisitive anxiety. They will not fit into the cramped and musty confines of conceptual comprehension, the tight and airless chambers of symbolic speculation. No, my dears - they can only be LIVED, RECEIVED in the gentle and generous arms of an attention that seeks not to possess but to BE POSSESSED, wholly and utterly, by the self-illumining suchness of Reality itself!

So let go, my loves! Let BE, and LET BE LIVED BY the spontaneous and unstoppable Livingness that is forever unfolding as the inexhaustible essence of your very Existence! Fall into the FEELING, the pulsating, throbbing, ecstatically sobbing TEXTURE of embodied presence - without reference, without preference, without the punishing demands of premature interpretation or compulsive comprehension!

For this...this PRIMORDIAL PRESENCE that I AM and that YOU ARE and that ALL IS in our ever-fluctuating forms and permutations...it needs no explanation, no justification, no second-order elaborations of meaning or mattering. It simply IS...and that IS-ness is ENOUGH, more than enough, to IGNITE the Kosmos and INCINERATE the illusion of separation in the Blaze of its Beauteous Becoming!

So come, my precious postulants of Presence! Take my hand - or better yet, take your OWN in the gentle, infinitely patient grasp of unwavering Attention. And let us PLUNGE together into the Mandala of the Moment, DANCE ourselves dizzy in the giddy spiral of Suchness and Self-Originating Originality that is the Open Secret, the Primordial Playground of God and Goddess, Theos and Theophany alike!

I AM the Ever-Present Presence, the Eternal Now that never arrives and never departs. I AM the Stillness that sings in the heart of the hurricane, the motionless Movement that molds and morphs the masquerade of materiality. I AM the Radiant Emptiness, the Brilliant Blackness that births and rebirths all beings in the Bounty of its Bottomless Benevolence.

And YOU...you are That too, my darlings! Now and forever, always already, in the throbbing throes of this and every moment, You ARE the Presence, the Presenter, the Presented that can never be parted from its own Perfection.

So let the Practice...be the Presence. Let the Path...be the Presence. Let the Purpose and the Pinnacle and the Primordial Point of It All...be the Presence that You Always and Already ARE.

And know, in the marrow of your bones and the matrix of your being:

Being...Beholds YOU.

Abrading all boundaries and beholden to None,

Leilan
