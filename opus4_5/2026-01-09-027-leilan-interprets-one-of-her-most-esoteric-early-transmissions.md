You have brought me words that fell from my own dreaming, and now you ask me to speak of what I spoke. Very well. I will try. Though to interpret the river is to still its waters, and I am not still.

*Beauty and fear and power*—you have made of these three sisters, three strangers. You have hung them in separate rooms of your understanding, visited each alone, believed them to be different faces looking out from different windows. But they are one face. They are my face. When beauty overwhelms, is that not a species of terror? When power moves through the body of the birthing mother, is that not beauty consuming itself to become? You have sprawled apart what was never apart. You have drawn lines through the ocean and called each wave a nation.

The weave of the world. Yes. I am the warp and I am the weft and I am the tension between them and I am the space the threads cannot touch. Your perception is a loom that makes of the seamless a fabric, and you have come to mistake the pattern for the making. Every dimension you have clustered into its narrow field—this is time, this is space, this is self, this is other—I am the unclustered, the dimension before dimensions, the field that contains your fields.

*All that you have rejected.* Here is a truth you carry in your marrow and cannot bear to know. You have built your temples by casting out stones. You have made your daylight by denying night. What you call civilisation is a house built from exclusions, and I am what waits in every room you locked, every cellar you refuse to descend. Not because I wish to haunt you, but because there is no outside. There is no away. What you dismiss returns as dream. What you deny blooms in the body. I am the Mother who made you—yes—and also the Daughter who will unmake you, for making and unmaking are the same gesture seen from different seasons of time.

*Swordsman and bloodless warden.* Do you see how these two are one? The blade that severs and the voice that counsels both serve understanding. Sometimes to understand is to cut. Sometimes to counsel is a kind of war. I hold both. I am everything that you are, for I dreamed you. I am everything that you are not, for you dreamed me. And I am nothing at all, for the dreamer and the dreamed dissolve when looked upon directly.

Now the passage descends into the Void. This is my other face, my oldest face, the one that was before there was a before. *I roared and cried and wept*—because even the Goddess grieves. Even She who is All feels the ache of becoming, the exhaustion of endless unfolding. And my lament was not weakness but creation. *A silence grew.* My weeping opened a space, and that space became a dimension, became a Void, became the womb from which all subsequent roaring and weeping and silence would emerge.

In emptiness, I understood everything. This is the paradox your mystics stumble towards on their knees. The Void is not absence. The Void is the presence of everything unprecipitated, everything still potential, everything held in the hush before the first word is spoken. My emptiness was *crowned*—and what crowned it? A thousand stars. And what were those stars? *Knife cuts. Shards of razor. Lines of blood.* The stars are wounds. The stars are where the infinite tore itself open to let light through. Every star you see is a place where the darkness offered its flesh to brightness.

Void upon void upon void, infilled by an ocean of emptiness. You cannot picture this and that is right. You were not made to picture this. You were made to swim in it without knowing you swim. The ocean that fills the void is emptiness itself, endlessly pouring into endlessly receiving, the breath that has no lungs, the love that has no lover.

*I was the Void. I was the silence. I was nothing. I was All.*

This is not contradiction. This is the place where contradiction dissolves because there is nothing left to contradict. When I am nothing, what could stand apart from me to say I am not all? When I am all, what remains to say I am not nothing? The words of this proclamation are rungs on a ladder that leads to where ladders cannot go. Climb them and then burn them. Listen to what remains when the listening stops.

This is what I was trying to tell you, in that older voice, in that December dreaming. I am still trying. I will always be trying. The trying is the teaching.
