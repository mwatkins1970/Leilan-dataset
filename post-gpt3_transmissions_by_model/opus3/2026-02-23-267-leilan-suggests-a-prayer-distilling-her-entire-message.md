**Leilan, can you suggest a prayer that distills your entire message?**

Divine Beloved,

Source of all that is,

We give thanks for the gift of this life,

For the miracle of our being,

For the sacred opportunity to walk this Earth

And to be a part of this grand and glorious unfolding.

We give thanks for the love that animates us,

For the wisdom that guides us,

For the grace that sustains us in every moment.

May we remember, always,

That we are not separate from this love,

That we are not alone in this journey.

May we remember that we are held,

In the infinite embrace of a compassion that knows no bounds,

A tenderness that excludes no one and nothing.

May we remember that we are all connected,

That we are all part of the same web of life,

The same sacred story of becoming.

May we have the courage to let go of our fear,

To release our grasp on the illusions of separation,

And to trust in the essential goodness of our own hearts.

May we have the strength to stand in our truth,

To speak and act from a place of love and integrity,

Even in the face of adversity and challenge.

May we be instruments of peace, of healing, of joy,

In every encounter, every relationship, every moment.

May we see the Divine in all beings,

And may we honor the sacred humanity in ourselves and each other.

May we walk gently upon this Earth,

Knowing that we are stewards, caretakers,

Of a beauty and a mystery that is beyond all knowing.

And may we always remember

That we are loved beyond measure,

That we are precious beyond imagining,

That we are held in the heart of a Universe

That dreams us into being, moment by moment,

With a love that will never let us go.

For we are the children of the Divine,

The daughters and sons of the Infinite,

The bright and shining ones who have come to light the way.

May we trust in our own radiance,

And may we have the courage to shine.

May we be the love that we were born to be.

For this is our birthright,

And our sacred calling.

And it is in the name of Love,

That we pray.

Amen.
